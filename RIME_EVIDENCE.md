# RIME Evidence

## Project
VELAN – Voice-Enabled Land & Agricultural Navigator

## Hard Voice Problem
Interruption and Recovery

## Voice Problem
In a normal voice interface, when the assistant is speaking,
the user may interrupt with new information. The system must
stop the outdated response, keep the conversation state
consistent, and respond to the updated request.

## Our Claim
VELAN can interrupt an active spoken response, discard the
outdated response, process the farmer's new request, and
provide the updated response without stale or overlapping audio.

## Acceptance Test

1. Start a normal voice interaction.
2. Ask VELAN a farming-related question.
3. Allow VELAN to begin speaking through Rime.
4. Interrupt VELAN while it is speaking.
5. Provide a new or changed request.
6. Verify that the old audio stops.
7. Verify that the outdated response does not continue.
8. Verify that the new request is processed.
9. Verify that the final spoken response matches the new request.
